Machine-readable list for [Legacy 1.16.5 estimate](../../../docs/wiki/Legacy-1.16.5-Estimate.md) (2026-11-24). The unresolved symbols were then walked along 1.16.5 to 1.18.2 to 1.19.2 to 1.20.1 by tools/compat/gap_stage_analyze.py.
