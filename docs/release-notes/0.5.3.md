## Legacy Connector 0.5.3

Fixes and additions since 0.5.2, most of them from player reports. Delete `.legacyconnector/cache` in your instance after updating.

### All legacy versions
- A save crash after opening a second world in one session (enchanted items failing to save) is fixed; several other pieces of state carried from one world to the next are now reset.
- Recipes that compute their own result (machine tiers, upgrades) stay live instead of being flattened; a Forge recipe form that was silently dropped is read correctly, which restores missing tool, armor and cart recipes in several mods.
- Mods' own item renderers get the texture they expect bound first (wrong or invisible item icons in inventories and JEI).
- New `/legacyconnector report` command: writes one zip with the logs and mod list (user name, home path and tokens removed) to attach to a GitHub issue.

### 1.7.10
- Thermal Expansion: machine crafting and upgrading, augment slots, item icons in Thermal GUIs.
- Witchery: the vampire ritual's skull is recognised. Thaumcraft: arriving in the Outer Lands next to the maze portal.
- Mystcraft: age skies no longer hide terrain beyond 100 blocks, and show after loading straight into an age.
- Air at Y=0 is no longer reported as bedrock to legacy mods (machines stopping at Y=0, nothing placeable there); only the exact origin keeps it for Traincraft's tracks.
- GTNH Mixins API, more LWJGL 2 and library members bridged; several more mods link.

### 1.12.2
- Thermal: tiered items keep their tier; the Redstone Furnace no longer stops after a restart.
- Galacticraft Legacy: spawn eggs, Buggy variants, planet gravity and sky, Space Race Manager button, staying on a planet after reload.
- GregTech CE (TJ Edition) loads (it needs Applied Energistics 2 installed); its generated blockstates resolve. Machine screens and ore generation do not work yet.
- World types of 1.12.2 mods are offered on the Create World screen (vanilla terrain with the mod's biomes for now).
- JEI: categories, recipe transfer, vanilla-tab recipes, mod ingredient types (gases, aspects) and ghost slots of legacy JEI plugins.
- Candy World, The Betweenlands, Colored Redstone, AVP/MDXLib, TerraFirmaCraft + Florae fixes.

### 1.2.5 to 1.6.4
- Ores generate in the deep layer (below Y=0) as well. Machines keep their contents across a reload (stacks saved by the mod's own id).
- BuildCraft: Quarry and Mining Well below Y=0, double chests, Assembly Table recipes in JEI.
- Railcraft: drag-splitting in GUIs, Steam Locomotive GUI and icon, wall textures, Rock Crusher and Rolling Machine in JEI.
- Xycraft: names, lights, Fire Basin, soil, Multi Tank GUI and walls, and a crash when breaking a tank block.
- IC2: rubber leaves name and texture; client copies of machines no longer simulate on their own.
- Witches and More: desert structures generate (old biome types `DESERT`, `FROZEN`, `WATER`).
- Jars built for another Minecraft version (1.6.2) are skipped with a clear reason.

### 1.20.1 (alpha)
- Cracker's Wither Storm: load failure fixed. FNAF Management Wanted: laptop previews, title screen, a per-tick server error. Magic Evolved / Photon effects draw (some particle kinds still missing).
- Table audit: every 1.20.1 mapping is checked against the 1.21.1 classes at build time.

### Beta 1.7.3
- More shim behaviour: options, input, explosions, textures uploaded by mods, minecarts and boats as extendable classes.

### Known issues
- IC2 1.4.7: a full, idle BatBox/MFE/MFSU still shows 0 in its GUI.
- Xycraft: the tank's forcefield overlay is missing. Thermal 1.7.10: cover items offset, Dynamo fuels not in JEI.
- Legacy world types show on the Create World screen but give vanilla terrain with the mod's biomes for now; 1.7.10 world types with their own generator are not verified yet.
- The report command is new: check the zip before attaching it.
