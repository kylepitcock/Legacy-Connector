## Legacy Connector 0.5.2

A hotfix for 0.5.1, with the fixes that reached the nightly since. Back up your worlds before updating; legacy saved data keeps its format.

### Regressions of 0.5.1, fixed

- **World generation could freeze.** When a legacy world generator's flowing liquid broke a block while a chunk was generating (seen with The Lion King, 1.6.4), the server hung. 0.5.1 made vanilla blocks answer "what do you drop" from the modern loot table, and that lookup touched the live world from a generation thread. It now only runs on the live world; during generation the block answers as it did in 0.5.0.
- **IC2's explosions could stop mid-tick** (the nuke in IC2 Classic 1.7.10): an empty loot roll put an empty entry in a block's drop list. Empty rolls are skipped.

### Also fixed since 0.5.1

**1.4.7 to 1.6.4**
- One world generator that writes far outside its chunks can no longer use up the whole budget for such writes and make every other generator's writes disappear (Witches and More's End island).

**1.5.2**
- Applied Energistics failed while loading when Universal Electricity was not installed: a check of an item's methods did not survive a signature naming a class of an absent optional mod.

**1.7.10**
- CustomOreGen (#82): veins are no longer cut off at y=192. The shifted ore pass now reports the shifted world height.
- HBM's Nuclear Tech (#330): the metal, office and bunker doors draw as doors, not as two full blocks. This covers every mod door with the vanilla door render type.
- Traincraft (#105): fluids of modern mods get their name, texture and tint in tankers and survive a reload.
- Witchery (#107): the mirror's reflection turns towards you.
- ProjectE (#112): EMC values for items made by vanilla recipes (planks, sticks, chests, tools). The recipe list legacy mods read now includes vanilla's crafting recipes. The Alchemical Bag shows its colour name.

**1.12.2**
- CustomOreGen (#295): veins are no longer cut off at y=257.
- Colored Redstone (#284): coloured repeaters, comparators and torches draw in their own colours. Blocks may have up to 512 models for states their metadata does not store (was 64).
- Kindred Legacy (#113): the potion powder recipes exist. Custom recipe ingredient types and potion ingredients in JSON recipes are read.
- Techguns CE, Thermal Expansion and more (contributed by TheSlize, #334): entity renderers for vanilla classes, models with a mod's private sprites, OBJ and Forge transform presets, item subtypes past metadata 15, hurt and death handling of entities a mod kills itself, tile entities of blocks placed during chunk generation, double chests, banners, raw ores counted as ores in the ore dictionary (`oreDictionary.rawOresAsOres`), modern smelting recipes in the furnace list legacy machines read.
- **JEI plugins of 1.12.2 mods run on modern JEI** (#334): their recipe categories, recipes, catalysts and click areas show up (Thermal Expansion's machines, Tinkers' Construct's smeltery, Mekanism). A mod's own ingredient types (Mekanism gases) are left out, as are recipe transfer and additions to vanilla categories. `bridges.jei1122.enabled=false` turns it off.
- A mod's direct `onDeath` call no longer kills a living player.
- A tile entity placed during chunk generation that marks itself dirty no longer reaches the live world from the generation thread.
- A mod's projectile that marks itself dead and then sends an effect to clients (firework bursts) no longer loses the effect.
- A projectile with a broken velocity can no longer hang the server tick.

### Known problems
- Thermal Expansion 1.4.7: Liquiduct fluids can turn invisible after a reload, and machines can lose their contents on save.
- 1.20.1: large packs (All the Mods 9) do not stay in a world yet.

Fixes keep arriving in the `nightly` pre-release between stable versions.
