## Legacy Connector 0.5.1

A bug-fix and compatibility release on top of 0.5.0. Back up your worlds before updating; legacy saved data keeps its format. Delete
`.legacyconnector/cache` once after updating if a mod behaves differently than in the nightly (translations are rebuilt automatically when
the layer's output changes).

### Fixed since 0.5.0

**Beta 1.7.3**
- Player momentum survives velocity changes, mounts ignore sneak, and mobs hosted from mods walk at the right speed.
- Bone meal works from a stack, and Beta dimensions get their biomes.
- Chest-like blocks render, blocks outline with their own selection boxes, container screens opened from ticks get a menu, and modifier keys work in screens.

**1.2.5**
- 1.2.5 jars are detected by their content wherever they sit (`mods/1.4.7/` no longer hides one) and are sorted into `mods/1.2.5/`. Failures name the jar as Minecraft 1.2.5.
- RedPower (also on 1.4.7, #310 #312): GUIs work, multipart blocks are picked part by part, tubes, pipes and covers are listed in the creative tab, and tile entities load.
- Dozens of old API members are implemented.

**1.4.7 to 1.6.4**
- IndustrialCraft 2 1.4.7 loads (#328).
- BuildCraft (#329):
  - Quarries keep running after a world reload. Forge chunk-loading tickets are now saved and handed back to every mod that loads chunks.
  - Vanilla blocks mined by mods drop according to the modern loot table, so no more cobblestone from granite or silk-touched ores.
- Railcraft carts construct (#323). Entity data ids that a mod registers twice no longer fail, which also fixes the Witches and More zombies.
- Xycraft torches draw as torches (#327).
- Village buildings of 1.4.7 and 1.5.2 mods are built whole (Forestry's apiarist house).
- Thermal Expansion 1.4.7 (#248): machines, energy cells, Tesseract and conduits draw, and NEI recipe handlers work through the NEI-to-JEI bridge.
- Forestry and Mystcraft 1.4.7-1.6.4: blocks without an item get one on demand (30 Forestry items were lost), and Mystcraft's postInit loads.
- Mods that never name a block register it as `block<id>` instead of colliding, and tile entities no longer lose data to a missing id.
- 1.6.4 world generators built from block ids look the block up when they generate, as 1.6.4 did. A decorator built before its block no longer fails in every chunk (#326).
- Equivalent Exchange 3 and Railcraft 1.6.4 initialise.

**1.7.10**
- Plants no longer drop in the dark or at night under a roof (#326):
  - Legacy light checks see the light without the night's darkening, as the old games did.
  - 1.7.10 plants test only their soil, as in Forge 1.7.10.
  - Flower generators find their spots while a chunk generates.
- Mods that build their renderers during preInit load on dedicated servers again. A renderer class needed a client-only class, so such mods were skipped as client-only.
- IC2 Classic cables drop when mined (#324). Blocks that allow harvesting by hand are respected.
- Thermal Expansion's Redstone Furnace and Sawmill know vanilla recipes (#293, #311).
- The Legends Mod (#299):
  - Suits are drawn at the mod's far third-person camera.
  - The camera lift comes before the camera backs away, so it no longer clips into hills.
- Vanilla item base classes get their creative tabs (NTM armour and weapons were missing, #321), and the inventory tabs of Galacticraft and Tinkers' Construct work (#318).
- Mystcraft chunk handling and faster saves (#306), sounds and GUI events, and more old API members.

**1.12.2**
- IC2 Classic 1.12.2 loads (#324): water and lava have their blocks, as in Forge.
- Thermal series (#311):
  - The Flux Capacitor no longer loses energy.
  - Multi-tier items show their tiers instead of all Basic.
  - Buckets fill the Portable Tank.
  - The Strongbox and cell and device frames draw in the inventory and JEI.
  - Vanilla smelting reaches the Redstone Furnace, and vanilla logs reach the Sawmill.
- Fossils and Archeology: Revival (#315): LLibrary's network wrapper is injected, a server crash after joining is fixed, and the main menu has its buttons.
- Colored Redstone (#284): blocks a mod registers under vanilla names exist (under the mod's namespace) and keep their colour.
- Mods' mixins on vanilla entity classes apply to the vanilla mobs (Wolf Armor and Storage, #282).
- Twilight Forest hanging entities and generation, the Chisel & Bits server crash, and the Botania/Astral baking crash are fixed. Techguns blocks are no longer registered twice. TF2 Stuff Reloaded ammo works (#184).
- CodeChickenLib loads beside the modern one, a missing required library is reported, and item and entity API additions were made.

**Forge 1.20.1**
- Four more rounds of API coverage: Ars Nouveau, Iron's Spells, Cyclops Core, Architectury, Refined Storage, Mekanism, ProjectRed, FTB Chunks, the Thermal series and others load or render further.
- Mixins repaired:
  - accessors of fields 1.21.1 removed (Photon, LDLib)
  - `@Inject` handlers on methods that gained parameters (LDLib's block entity saving)
- Wither Storm and LDLib start-up fixes (#320 #322).
- Palladium and Alien Evolution runtime fixes and a read-only preflight analyzer, contributed by kentvillamor08.
- Terralith-style 1.20.x data-pack jars are detected and converted.

**General**
- Faster launches:
  - Jar digests are remembered between launches (warm loader phase about 5 s -> 1.2 s in a test set).
  - The legacy block flattening table is cached (about 1 s -> 20 ms).
  - 1.20.1 translation uses less CPU.
- The compatibility site was redesigned: one search over every version, a page per mod with all its results, and a report link for each version.
- A boot crash when another mod (Sinytra Connector) loaded a NeoForge class early is fixed (#311), and the three "mixin not found" errors at every start are gone (#310).
- Legacy ores below y=0 come from one shifted full-height pass (`deepLayer.mode=shifted`, #82 #295; `split` is the old scheme).
- Libraries that a modern mod nests under `META-INF` no longer clash with the legacy copy (C2ME).
- Tile entities and entities that cannot write their NBT no longer fail chunk saves.
- Hooks that need MixinExtras 0.5 sit in an optional mixin config, so an older NeoForge 21.1 skips only those.

### Known problems
- Thermal Expansion 1.4.7: Liquiduct fluids can turn invisible after a reload, and machines can lose their contents on save.
- 1.20.1: large packs (All the Mods 9) do not stay in a world yet. A client can hang at world load with some mods (seen with Deeper Darker or Another Furniture).
- Legacy ores stop below the modern world top with some CustomOreGen configurations (#82 #295, being worked on).

Fixes keep arriving in the `nightly` pre-release between stable versions.
